## D2 rule
Canonical = the dominant signature of each role unless the design boards say otherwise; every other signature in the role becomes either the canonical one (a drift fix, listed in `expected-<step>.md`) or a named variant of the primitive. Semantic tokens added in `theme.css` on top of the palette: `--btn-primary-bg/-ink/-bg-hover/-disabled-bg/-disabled-ink`, `--pill-bg/-ink/-border/-on-bg/-on-ink/-on-border/-border-hover`, `--input-bg/-border/-border-focus/-ink/-placeholder`, `--row-hover`, `--row-selected`, `--card-bg/-border/-border-hover/-bg-hover`, `--menu-bg/-border/-shadow/-item-hover`, `--modal-bg/-border/-shadow`, `--tag-*`, `--font-body/-display/-mono`, `--t-10…--t-30`.
